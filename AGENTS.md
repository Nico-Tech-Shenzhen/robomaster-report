# Scramble RoboMaster research report

- Write reader-facing report prose in an academic, concrete, reader-first style.
- Never delete detailed research merely to shorten the report.
- Use university names as prose subjects; team names are secondary identifiers.
- `GLOBAL_REVIEW.md` archives the latest report-wide review; `REPORT_PLAN.md` and `CROSS_CHAPTER_MATRIX.md` are its compact operational routing; `dic.md` is the terminology authority.
- Kimi source cards are evidence archives, not report prose. Strong claims must respect `research/china-2026/claims-audit.md`.
- Do not rewrite the entire report in one task. Work chapter-by-chapter unless explicitly instructed otherwise.
- Use the appropriate repository Skill for chapter editing, media, history recovery, and QA.
- Do not commit or push unless explicitly requested.

## Scramble internal review phase

- Research and drafting are frozen for internal review. The complete PDF has been visually reviewed and approved by the human reviewer. Do not perform another broad rewrite or repeat full visual review merely to enter this phase.
- Preserve chapter architecture unless a reviewer identifies a substantive problem. Make targeted corrections; do not regenerate chapters. Preserve verified research evidence and citations.
- GitHub issues and pull-request reviews are the review record. Link corrections to the applicable issue or review where available; local status notes do not replace that record.
- Update the PDF after meaningful review batches, not after every minor edit.
- Do not add the final contributor table during internal review. Only after internal review is complete, inspect issue authors, substantive issue comments, PR authors, PR reviewers, commits, and other explicitly documented research/review contributions.
- At that final stage, prepare a proposed table titled 「執筆・調査・査読メンバー」 with columns 氏名, 所属, 主な担当・貢献. Do not infer real names, affiliations, or roles from GitHub usernames alone; flag unresolved identities for human confirmation.
- Record actual contributions accurately; do not equate every GitHub interaction with the same level of authorship. The contributor table is the final editorial update before publication.
