## Verified Facts

### Application
- Replace traditional "aluminum brick" DC-DC modules for **NUC mini-PC power supply** in RM robots (24 V → 20 V).
- Goals: smaller volume, higher current, lower ripple, lower cost.

### Electrical specs
| Parameter | Value | Conditions |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Input voltage | 6–36 V | Module uses 26 V TVS; remove/replace if input > 26 V |
| Output voltage | 0.5–28 V | Set by resistor divider |
| Max output current | 10 A | Depends on MOSFET, heatsink, OCP sense resistor |
| Peak efficiency | **98.1 %** | Author claim |
| Ripple | **77 mVpp** | @ 20 V / 7 A output |

### Key components
- **Controller:** MPS MP2980GR synchronous four-switch buck-boost controller.
  - Buck mode: valley-current control.
  - Boost mode: peak-current control.
  - FCCM + configurable average current limit.
  - Configurable OCP, OVP, UVLO hysteresis.
- **MOSFET:** CSD18540 ("淘宝三毛管") or BSC028N06NS.
- **Inductor:** 10 µH 1770 (≈3.5 RMB).
- **Capacitor:** 100 µF 35 V solid electrolytic (≈0.64 RMB).
- **Cost:** < 55 RMB total per unit; lower at volume.

### PCB design notes
- Large copper pour on high-current paths; minimal loop obstruction.
- FB divider trace must be **short**; voltage sense taken **after output capacitor** (CO).
- Reserved heatsink mounting holes (35 mm × 35 mm Al or Cu heatsink, 29 mm hole spacing).
- 4-layer PCB, JLCPCB free-process compatible.

### Thermal / noise evidence
- Ripple measured at 77 mVpp under 20 V / 7 A load.
- Author recommends adding heatsink for sustained > 5 A operation.
- No galvanic isolation (non-isolated buck-boost).

### Observed limitations
- MP2980 is QFN-32 (4 mm × 4 mm); author notes soldering difficulty and recommends reflow sequence: MP2980 first, then top SMD, bottom SMD, through-hole.
- TVS must be selected for actual input voltage; default 26 V TVS must be removed/replaced if supply exceeds 26 V.
