# 从赛场到课堂 (From the Arena to the Classroom) — Exhibition Panel Evidence

## Overview

The "从赛场到课堂" exhibition was displayed at the RoboMaster 2026 National Final
venue (Shenzhen, August 2026). It showcased 15 universities that have integrated
RoboMaster competition participation into formal education, curriculum, laboratory
systems, or industry-university collaboration.

**Important**: The claims below are transcribed from exhibition panels (field
evidence). They do NOT constitute verified primary documentation. Each claim is
flagged with its current evidence level. Follow-up searches are required to
confirm formal curriculum status, syllabi, credit hours, etc.
