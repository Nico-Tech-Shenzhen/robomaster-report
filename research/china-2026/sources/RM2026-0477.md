### Education Model — STRONG PRIMARY EVIDENCE
The article explicitly describes a formal, structured education system:

**Four-level progressive competition training system**:
1. **校内赛（大一）** — Intramural competition (Year 1/freshman)
2. **联盟赛（大二）** — League competition (Year 2/sophomore)
3. **超级对抗赛（大三）** — Super对抗赛/RMUC (Year 3/junior)
4. **人工智能挑战赛（大四、研究生）** — AI Challenge (Year 4/senior + graduate)

**Multi-dimensional progressive practice teaching platform**:
- "课程实验-企业实践-科技创新-科研训练"
  (Course experiments — Enterprise practice — Technology innovation — Research training)

**Academic program support**:
- The base and competition support the construction of:
  - 机器人工程 (Robot Engineering) new engineering major
  - 人工智能 (Artificial Intelligence) new engineering major
