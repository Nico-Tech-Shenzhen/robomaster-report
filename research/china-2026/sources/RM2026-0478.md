### Laboratory Context
- Team is part of the 机械与汽车工程学院实验教学中心 (Mechanical and Automotive
  Engineering Experimental Teaching Center)
- Center has industry collaborations:

  - PTC (数字化产品创新平台)
  - UG (制造业数字化联合实验室)
- Center received 300+万元 for undergraduate experimental teaching equipment
