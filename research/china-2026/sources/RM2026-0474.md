### TARS-GO Team Profile (from 2020-10-28 article)
- Founded end of 2018
- First competition entry won national first prize (top 16)
- Cross-campus, cross-school, cross-disciplinary, cross-major platform
- Student-led with faculty guidance
- Built 5 complete robots: infantry, sentry, hero, engineer, drone
- Explored "intelligent machines and systems" cross-disciplinary training system
- Aims to become "cross-disciplinary, international new engineering open experiment platform"

### Science Education Outreach (2026-08-17)
- "吉甲智创"科创美育研学实践活动 held for middle school students
- JLU Communication Engineering students served as science popularization guides
- Activities: robot operation experience, naked-eye 3D light field display
- Held in 吉甲大师双创实践基地
- Media: 中国青年报 (China Youth Daily) reported
