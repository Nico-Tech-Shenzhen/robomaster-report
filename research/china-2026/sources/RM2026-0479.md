## Key Findings

### Team Identity
- **Team Name**: Robot Pilots (note: article spells "Robot Polits" which is a typo)
- **University**: 深圳大学 (Shenzhen University)
- **Competition**: ICRA 2018 DJI RoboMaster AI Challenge
- **Result**: 季军 (3rd place / bronze)
- **Location**: Brisbane, Australia

### Competition Context
- ICRA = IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation
- AI Challenge: fully autonomous robots shooting against DJI official robots
- 19 teams from global universities including:
  - Johns Hopkins University (USA)
  - Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology (USA)
  - University of Alberta (Canada)
  - University of Melbourne (Australia)
  - Nanyang Technological University (Singapore)
  - Hong Kong University of Science and Technology
  - Zhejiang University, HIT, Shenzhen University, Xi'an Jiaotong, Wuhan University,
    South China University of Technology, Northeastern University, etc.

### University Support (verified)
- Supported by 深圳大学高水平大学建设之"国际化行动计划"
  (Shenzhen University High-Level University Construction "Internationalization Action Plan")
- Plan actively funds outstanding undergraduates to participate in international
  competitions and academic exchanges

### K-12 Collaboration (verified)
- April 2018: President 李清泉 signed cooperation agreement with Shenzhen Middle School
  to jointly create "空间智能创新实验室" (Spatial Intelligence Innovation Laboratory)
- SZU RobotPilots team participated in ICRA 2018 together with Shenzhen Middle School students
- Aims to strengthen high-quality education resource sharing and cultivate
  internationally-minded innovative talent
