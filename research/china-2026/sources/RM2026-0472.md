### Formal Course Details
- **Course Code**: C3000000119
- **Course Title**: 《机器人创新设计》(Robot Innovation Design)
- **Course Type**: 选修课 (elective course)
- **Target Students**: 东北大学在校本科生 (NEU undergraduate students)

### Course Content
- Multi-disciplinary comprehensive course covering:

  - Computer science
  - Sensors
  - Artificial intelligence
  - Electronic technology
  - Mechanical engineering
- Based at 东北大学机器人科普基地 (NEU Robot Science Popularization Base), which is a
  辽宁省科普基地 (Liaoning Province Science Popularization Base)
- Course aims to develop students' robot design hands-on ability and innovation capability
