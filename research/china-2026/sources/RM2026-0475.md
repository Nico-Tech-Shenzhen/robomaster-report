## Key Findings

### Reborn Team (from official PDF)
- **Team Name**: 北京科技大学Reborn机器人竞技团队
- **Founded**: 2018年
- **Size**: 30余人 (30+ members)
- **Type**: 技术创新团队 (technology innovation team)
- **Competitions**: RoboMaster, Robocon, and related competitions
- **Advisor**: 王旭 (Wang Xu)
- **Location**: 北京科技大学学生创新创业基地 (USTB Student Innovation-Entrepreneurship Base)

### Student Innovation-Entrepreneurship Base
- **Affiliation**: 高等工程师学院工程训练中心 (School of Advanced Engineering,
  Engineering Training Center)
- **Labs included**:
  - 学科交叉创新实验室 (Interdisciplinary Innovation Lab)
  - 数码设计实验室 (Digital Design Lab)
  - 机器人实验室 (Robot Lab)
  - 机器人招聘 (likely typo for 机器人竞培 / robot competition training)
  - Reborn机器人竞技团队 (Reborn Robot Competition Team)
- **Purpose**: Provide open environment, standardized management, encourage
  innovation, rich resources for student innovation practice
- Each lab has one full-time experimental teacher

### USTB Innovation System (from 2014 and 2019 quality reports)
- **科技创新作为必修环节纳入本科教学计划** (Science/technology innovation is a
  REQUIRED component in the undergraduate teaching plan)
- **Minimum 2 credits** required for all students
- **8 school-level innovation labs**, ~2000 sqm total area
- **1500+ students/year** served with innovation practice projects and training
- **70%+ student coverage** in innovation/entrepreneurship competitions
- **30%+ award rate** in competitions
- **200+ innovation mentors** (internal + external entrepreneurs + alumni)
- **167 industry-university collaboration bases** with 100+ enterprises and local governments
- **21 incubation teams** in 2019

### Mechanical Engineering School Robot Programs
- Research areas: industrial robots, teaching robots, entertainment robots,
  service robots
- Launched: large humanoid performance robots, biomimetic robot fish,
  biomimetic marine life evolution demonstration platform
- Faculty: 乔红 (Qiao Hong), Dean, Professor, National Distinguished Young
  Scientist, IEEE RAS Administrative Committee member
- Research: robot "hand-eye-brain" (manipulation, vision, brain-inspired AI)
