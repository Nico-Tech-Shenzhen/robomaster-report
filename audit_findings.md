- Retained from `472be96` and the current working revision: 2026 rule versions and team implementations, roster distinctions, precise sponsor quantities, procurement caveats, government-role corrections, Japanese normalization, validators, MkDocs navigation, and PDF tooling.

### Material intentionally not restored

- Fixed four-year student role ladder: contradicted by the documented variation in current rosters.
- Huaqiangbei-centered same-day procurement and a Shenzhen competitive advantage: no adequate primary evidence.
- “Government withdrawal,” “graduation from the whitelist,” or a four-stage autonomy narrative: chronology existed, but the claimed causality was unsupported.
- Community-wide claims that ROS 2 Humble, YOLO, specific LiDAR models, or GitHub publication are universal standards: repository examples do not establish adoption rates.
- Cumulative startup, patent, investment, education-outcome, salary, and team-size totals derived from non-public material, promotional reporting, or incompatible definitions.
- Claims that Bambu Lab, Mammotion, Damiao, or Standard Robots are RoboMaster-founded companies without person-level participation evidence.
- Old rule dimensions, hardware limits, and early 2026 manual values superseded by later official versions.
- The former Japan-recommendation chapter: outside the current report purpose and heavily dependent on generalized causal claims.

## Current evidence limitations

- Public 2025–2026 rosters rarely include academic year for every member. Chapter 5 therefore presents documented team examples rather than a nationwide age distribution.
- No primary evidence was found for a Huaqiangbei-centered RoboMaster procurement system or a measurable Shenzhen-university advantage. The claim was removed.
- Sponsor support is documented for named teams and official partner programs, but jersey logos alone are not used to infer support type or amount.
- The cause of historical organizer and whitelist changes is not documented in the sources reviewed. Chapter 8 reports institutional facts without a causal phase narrative.
- Cost, team size, and working-time comparisons across RoboMaster, ROBOCON, and ROBOTAC remain unsuitable without a common dataset.
