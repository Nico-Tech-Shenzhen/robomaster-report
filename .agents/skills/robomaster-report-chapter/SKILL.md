# One-chapter editing

Treat `AGENTS.md` as automatically supplied project context. For the target chapter:

1. Read its section and evidence map in `REPORT_PLAN.md`, its row in `CROSS_CHAPTER_MATRIX.md`, and its row in `REVISION_STATUS.md`.
2. Read only applicable policy sections in `RULES.md` and terminology rows in `dic.md`. Read the relevant `MEDIA_PLAN.md` entry when adding or citing media.
3. Do not load all of `GLOBAL_REVIEW.md` by default. Use it only when the compact plan/matrix is ambiguous, when resolving a cross-chapter conflict, or when explicitly asked to apply the complete global review.
4. Begin research orientation with `research/china-2026/CODEX-HANDOFF.md`, `corpus-integrity.md`, and relevant parts of `claims-audit.md`. Read `evidence-clusters.md` when selecting evidence.
5. Use mapped evidence IDs. Consult `source-map.md`, one relevant thematic summary, `INDEX.md`, and individual cards only as needed. Never load all source cards by default.
6. Open a source card only when its claim will be used, its confidence needs checking, or citation/media metadata is required. Verify strong claims against `claims-audit.md`.
7. Recover history only for a defined missing topic. Draft reader-first academic prose with claim-level citations and planned tables/media, respecting the chapter's ownership and handoff boundaries.
8. Edit no unrelated chapter. Run chapter QA, update only the target `REVISION_STATUS.md` row, and flag the scheduled integration checkpoint when the chapter completes a drafting phase.

Preserve detailed supported research. Use university names as prose subjects and team names as secondary identifiers. Chapter 1 remains provisional until Chapters 2–9 are substantially stable. Do not commit or push without explicit instruction.
